🌟 Welcome to PowerPulse & Electricity Basics

PowerPulse is an intelligent electricity consumption monitoring, prediction, and anomaly detection system. It translates physical electricity meter readings into actionable financial and operational insights.

Core Electricity Concepts Explained for Beginners
Kilowatt-Hour (kWh)

The standard unit of electrical energy. 1 kWh = 1,000 Watts used continuously for 1 hour. For instance, running a 1,000W heater for 1 hour consumes 1 kWh.

Tariff Rate (₹ / kWh)

The cost charged by your electricity utility per single unit (kWh) consumed. PowerPulse defaults to ₹8.50 per unit, and can be customized per reading.

Delta Calculation

Cumulative meter meters only go forward. Your net consumption is simply:
Current Reading - Previous Reading = Net kWh

What PowerPulse Does Automatically

When you enter or upload readings, PowerPulse immediately calculates the consumed kWh, computes the financial cost in Indian Rupees (₹), flags any unusual surges with machine learning, and feeds the time-series forecasting model.

📝 How to Log Daily Meter Readings

If you inspect your electricity meter daily or weekly, logging readings in PowerPulse takes under 15 seconds.

1
Navigate to the Log Form

Click Readings in the navigation bar, then click the blue "+ Log Reading" button.

Open Log Reading Form
2
Enter Reading Details

You have two easy options:

Recommended
Meter Digits Method

Enter the Previous Reading (prefilled automatically from your last entry) and the Current Reading. Net kWh is calculated automatically.

Alternative
Direct Net Units Method

If your sub-meter or smart plug already tells you that you consumed 35.5 kWh today, simply type 35.5 directly into Net Consumption (kWh).

3
Confirm Tariff Rate & Facility

Check the Tariff Rate (₹ per kWh) (defaults to 8.50) and select or type your Building / Facility Name (e.g. "Main Residence", "Floor 2", "Workshop"). Click Save Reading Record.

Validation Guard

PowerPulse prevents human typing mistakes: your Current Reading cannot be lower than the Previous Reading. If entered incorrectly, the form alerts you before saving.

What Happens Right After Ingestion?
  1. De-duplication: If a reading already exists for that date and facility, it is safely updated rather than duplicated.
  2. Instant Cost Calculation: Multiplies kWh by the ₹ tariff to compute estimated cost.
  3. Auto-Anomaly Scan: Evaluates every uploaded reading against historical distributions to flag surges immediately.
  4. Model Ready: Navigate to AI Forecast to train a projection on your new data.

🧾 Utility Bills & Auditing Rate Inflation

Most people only look at the total amount on their monthly electricity bill. PowerPulse audits the effective cost per unit to reveal stealthy utility tariff increases.

Recording a Monthly Bill

When your utility company delivers your monthly statement, navigate to Bills → + Add Monthly Bill. Enter:

  • Billing Month: Any date in the billing cycle (e.g. Oct 2026).
  • Units Consumed: Total kWh billed by the company.
  • Total Bill Amount (₹): Total final payable amount.
Effective Rate Detection

The system calculates:
Effective Cost / Unit = Bill Amount (₹) / Units Billed

If your usage remained constant at 1,000 kWh, but your cost-per-unit jumped from ₹8.50 to ₹9.80, PowerPulse makes that inflation visible at a glance.

🤖 AI Consumption Forecasting

Plan budgets and identify unusual future consumption with PowerPulse's time-series regression engine.

How the Machine Learning Engine Works

Unlike basic averages, the predictor engineers rich temporal features from your recorded data:

Calendar Signals

Day of week, day of month, month, and weekend status capture recurring weekly habits.

Auto-Regressive Lags

Lag 1 (yesterday), Lag 2 (2 days ago), and Lag 7 (same day last week) track sequential inertia.

Rolling Averages

7-day moving window smooths random daily noise to reveal underlying facility trajectory.

Model Options & Accuracy Scorecards
  • Random Forest Regressor (Default): Captures complex non-linear equipment interactions and surge boundaries.
  • Ridge Regression: L2-regularized linear model that provides a fast, stable baseline.
  • RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error): Penalizes large errors heavily; lower is better.
  • MAE (Mean Absolute Error): Average expected deviation in kWh (e.g. 2.9 kWh).

🛡️ Surge & Anomaly Detection

Unflagged equipment malfunctions (like faulty AC thermostats, water heater leaks, or industrial compressors stuck running overnight) waste huge amounts of electricity.

The Dual-Layer Detection Strategy
Layer 1 — Isolation Forest

An unsupervised ensemble algorithm that builds isolation decision trees. Points requiring unusually few splits to isolate are classified as anomalous spikes.

Layer 2 — 2.5σ Statistical Limits

Calculates rolling mean and standard deviation. Any single day exceeding 2.5 standard deviations from the user's historical baseline triggers an immediate red flag.

Diagnostic Attribution

PowerPulse doesn't just display a warning number; it translates mathematical outliers into human-readable diagnostics:

• "Severe consumption surge: 96.20 kWh (2.7x user average)"
• "Significant load spike: 88.40 kWh (2.5x user average)"
• "Abnormally low consumption: 7.10 kWh (Possible vacancy or sub-meter disconnection)"
